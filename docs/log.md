# Weekly log

One short entry per person per week. Takes two minutes and does two jobs: keeps the
group in sync, and gives each of us something concrete to point at in the Stage III
peer evaluation and self-reflection.

The self-reflection quiz is marked on specificity, so an entry like "found the
studentAssessment join needs to go through assessments" is worth more later than
"worked on data".
